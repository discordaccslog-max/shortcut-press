Common requests

  • A download link didn't arrive. Check your spam folder first, then get in touch with your order number and the link will be resent. See the Delivery Policy.
  • A refund. Any purchase can be refunded within 14 days, no reason needed. Include your order number. See the Refund Policy.
  • A damaged file. Describe what happens when you open it, and a replacement will be sent if you prefer one to a refund.
  • Updated files. When a guide is revised for a software update, updated files are free to anyone who bought it. Include your order number.
  • A group license. For teachers, schools and companies. Include your organization's name and roughly how many people would use the guides.
  • Your personal information. Requests to access, correct or delete your information are handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
