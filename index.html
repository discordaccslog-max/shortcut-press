02/In every guide

What's inside each .zip

Every guide is built the same way. The PDF explains each skill and breaks it into numbered steps with annotated illustrations. Then you practice in an exercise and check your work against a worked solution that goes through every step.

The practice files mean you never start from a blank screen, and never practice on your own work.

05/Questions

Before you buy

The questions people ask most. Every other answer is on the FAQ page.

What exactly do I receive after purchase, and how?

You receive one .zip file. For a single guide, it contains the PDF guide and the practice files its exercises use. For the Complete Software Skills Library, it contains all seven guides, each in its own folder. No software, license key, account or physical item is included, and nothing is shipped.

Checkout is handled by our payment provider. Immediately after your payment is confirmed, the download link appears on the order confirmation screen and is emailed to the address you used at checkout. Select the link to download the .zip, then unzip it. The Delivery Policy goes through each step.

Is the software included?

No. The guides do not include any software. Each guide's page has a "Software required" section that names the program the guide uses. You need your own copy or access, obtained separately from the software's maker, and your use of it is governed by that maker's own terms.

Is there video or tutoring?

No. The guides are self-study material. There is no video, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work. You read each step, carry it out in the software, then do the exercises and check your work against the worked solutions in the guide.

What is the refund policy?

You can get a full refund on any purchase if you ask within 14 days. No reason is needed, and nothing needs to be returned. Contact us through the Contact page with your order number. Refunds go to your original payment method and are issued within 5 business days of your request; your payment provider's processing time may add a few days. See the Refund Policy.
