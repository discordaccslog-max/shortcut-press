Starting a Community Garden
	A plan for the empty lot on Maple Street
Why a garden here
	The lot has been empty for six years
	Twenty-three neighbors signed up in one week
	The nearest grocery store is two miles away
What we need
	Permission from the city
	Raised beds and soil
	A water connection
	Volunteers for the first season
The first season
	March: clear the lot and build beds
	April: plant greens and herbs
	June: first harvest
What it will cost
	Beds and soil: 1,800
	Tools and a shed: 950
	Water connection: 600
How you can help
	Adopt a bed
	Join a Saturday work party
	Tell a neighbor
