About

About Shortcut Press

Shortcut Press makes interactive, self-paced guides to software for spreadsheets, documents, 3D modeling, video editing, interface design and illustration.

What we make

#

Shortcut Press makes interactive software skills guides that run in your web browser. Each guide takes one program and works through it lesson by lesson: every lesson explains a skill, breaks it into numbered steps with an original diagram, checks your understanding with a short quiz, and ends with a practice exercise and a worked solution. Practice files, a keyboard shortcut trainer and progress tracking come with every guide, and the spreadsheet guides add a formula sandbox you can type into.

There are seven guides, each covering one program, and a library that unlocks all seven with a single license. You can see the full list on the home page.

Why written, interactive guides

#

A written step stays put while you carry it out. You can read it, try it in the program, read it again, and move on when you're ready, without scrubbing back through a recording. Quizzes and exercises turn reading into doing, and the worked solutions show every step, not just the final answer.

Working on real files matters too. That's why every exercise comes with its own practice file, so you never start from an empty screen and never practice on your own work.

How a lesson is put together

#
  • Overview. What the skill is, with a diagram that shows the idea.
  • Steps. Numbered instructions naming the menus and commands, with keyboard shortcuts shown the way you press them, like Ctrl Z.
  • Check your understanding. A short quiz with an explanation for every answer.
  • Practice. An exercise using a practice file, followed by a worked solution.

Your data stays with you

#

Your licenses and progress are stored only in your own browser. Nothing you do in a guide is sent to us or anyone else. You can export a backup at any time from My Guides.

What we don't do

#

The guides are self-study material. There are no video lessons, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work. The guides don't include any software, and they don't come with certificates.

Keeping guides current

#

Software makers update their programs, and sometimes an update moves a menu or renames a button. When an update changes the steps in a guide, the guide is revised, and you see the revised version the next time you open it.

Independent

#

Shortcut Press is independent. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any software maker. Software names appear on this site only to identify the programs the guides cover.

Get in touch

#

Questions about a guide, an order or a group license are welcome. Contact us through the Contact page.
