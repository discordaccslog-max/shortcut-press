FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Licenses, devices, backups, offline use, software, refunds and licensing for groups. If your question isn't answered here, contact us through the Contact page.

Buying and delivery

#
What exactly do I receive after purchase, and how?

You receive a license code for an interactive guide that runs in your web browser. Immediately after payment, you're shown your order confirmation page, which issues the code, activates it in that browser, and has an Open my guide button that takes you straight into the first lesson. You can also copy the code, save the confirmation as a PDF, or print it. Nothing is shipped. The Delivery Policy goes through each step.

What's inside a guide?

A series of lessons, each with step-by-step instructions, an original diagram, a short quiz and a practice exercise with a worked solution. Every guide also has downloadable practice files, a keyboard shortcut trainer for its program, and progress tracking with a Resume button. The two spreadsheet guides add a formula sandbox you can type formulas into. The Complete Software Skills Library unlocks all seven guides with one code.

Is there video or tutoring?

No. The guides are self-study material. There are no video lessons, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work. You read each step, carry it out in the software, then check your understanding and compare your work with the worked solutions.

Licenses and devices

#
How do licenses work?

Each purchase gives you a license code that starts with SCUT. The code is checked in your browser and unlocks the guide you bought; a Complete Software Skills Library code unlocks all seven guides. Activated licenses are saved in your browser on that device. Capitals, spaces and dashes don't matter when you type a code.

How do I use my guide on another device?

Open any page of this site on the other device, select My Guides in the bottom-left corner, and enter the same license code under Add a license. The same code works on all your devices. Progress is saved separately on each device; to move your progress, export a backup on one device and import it on the other.

I've lost my license code. What should I do?

If you still have a device where the guide is activated, open My Guides there: the code is listed under License details. Otherwise, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and the email address you used at checkout, and we'll send your code again.

How do backups work?

Your licenses and progress are stored only in your own browser, so clearing your browser's data removes them. In My Guides, Export backup saves a small file with all your licenses and progress; Import backup restores it on any device. If you haven't made a backup in 30 days, My Guides reminds you.

Can I use the guides offline?

Yes. After a guide has been opened once in a browser, it keeps working on that device without an internet connection. Practice files you've already downloaded are available too. Updates to the site and the guides are picked up automatically the next time you're online.

Which devices are supported?

Any computer, tablet or phone with a current version of a common web browser, such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, with JavaScript and site storage allowed. You can also install the site as an app from your browser's menu. To follow the steps you'll need the software the guide teaches, which most people use on a computer.

Software and updates

#
Is the software included?

No. The guides don't include any software. Each product page has a “Software required” section naming the program the guide uses. You obtain it separately from its maker, and your use of it is governed by the maker's own terms.

Which software versions are the guides written for?

The current versions of each program, using features that have stayed stable across recent versions. Software makers update their programs over time, so a menu may move or a button may be renamed between versions. Where steps differ between programs, such as Excel and Google Sheets, the guides show both.

What happens when the software updates?

When an update changes the steps in a guide, the guide is revised. You see the revised version automatically the next time you open the guide, at no extra cost.

Choosing a guide

#
Which guide should I start with?

Start with the program you already use or plan to use; each guide is complete on its own. If you're new to spreadsheet formulas, start with Spreadsheet Formulas & Functions before Data Analysis with Pivot Tables & Charts, which assumes you're comfortable with basic formulas and cell references.

What do the levels mean?

Beginner guides assume no experience with the program. Beginner to intermediate guides start from the basics and go on to techniques for people who already know their way around. Intermediate guides assume you're comfortable with the program's basics.

Licensing and refunds

#
Can teachers, schools or companies use the guides with a group?

A standard purchase is a personal license for one person. It doesn't cover use with a class, students, coworkers or a team. If you'd like to use the guides with a group, contact us through the Contact page about a group license, and include your organization's name and roughly how many people would use them.

Can I share my license code?

No. License codes may not be shared, published, sold or transferred. Each person who uses a guide needs their own purchase or a group license.

What is the refund policy?

You can get a full refund on any purchase if you ask within 14 days, with no reason needed. Contact us through the Contact page with your order number. Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 business days of your request; your payment provider's processing time may add a few days. The license ends when a purchase is refunded. See the Refund Policy.
