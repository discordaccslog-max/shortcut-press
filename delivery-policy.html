Delivery Policy

All products are digital downloads. Here is how delivery works.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short

  • All products are digital and delivered by download as a .zip file.
  • Your download link is emailed immediately after payment and shown on the order confirmation screen.
  • No email within an hour? Check your spam folder, then get in touch and the link will be resent.
  • There is no physical shipment.

Digital delivery only

Every Shortcut Press product is a digital download delivered as a single .zip file. Each guide's .zip contains a PDF guide and the practice files its exercises use. The Complete Software Skills Library .zip contains all seven guides, each in its own folder.

When you receive your download

As soon as your payment goes through, a download link is sent to the email address you used at checkout. The same link is shown on the order confirmation screen, so you can start your download straight away.

If the email doesn't arrive

If the email hasn't arrived within an hour, check your spam or junk folder first. If it isn't there, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and the email address you used at checkout, and the link will be resent.

Opening your files

Unzip the download to a folder on your computer. You'll need a PDF reader to open the guide, and the software the guide covers to open the practice files. The software is not included with any product; see the "Software required" section on each guide's page.

Updated files

When a guide is revised because a software update changed the steps, anyone who bought that guide can request the updated files free of charge. Contact us through the Contact page with your order number.

No physical shipment

Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping charges. Applicable taxes are calculated at checkout.

Questions

For anything else about delivery, contact us through the Contact page.
