Delivery Policy

All products are digital. Here is exactly how delivery works.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short

#
  • Every product is digital: an interactive guide that runs in your web browser, unlocked with a license code. There is no physical shipment.
  • Your license code is issued on the order confirmation page immediately after payment, and activated in that browser automatically.
  • To use your guide on another device, enter the same code in My Guides there.
  • Lost your license? Contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we'll send it again.

What you receive

#

Each guide is a series of interactive lessons in your browser, with step-by-step instructions, diagrams, quizzes, exercises with worked solutions, practice files to download, a keyboard shortcut trainer and progress tracking. The spreadsheet guides also include a formula sandbox. You receive one license code per purchase; a Complete Software Skills Library code unlocks all seven guides. No software, physical item or account is included.

How delivery works, step by step

#
  1. Agree and pay. On the guide's page, tick the box to agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, then select the buy button. You pay on our payment provider's secure checkout, where applicable taxes are shown.
  2. Your confirmation page opens. Immediately after payment, you're shown your order confirmation page. It issues your license code and shows the product, the date and time, and your order reference when the payment provider supplies one.
  3. Your license is activated in that browser. The confirmation page activates the code automatically in the browser you bought with. You can copy the code, save the confirmation as a PDF, or print it.
  4. Open your guide. The Open my guide button takes you straight into the first lesson.
  5. Use it on other devices. On any other computer, tablet or phone, open My Guides (the button in the bottom-left corner of every page) and enter the same license code.

When delivery happens

#

Immediately after your payment is confirmed. There is no waiting period, and nothing is prepared or sent by hand.

Where your license and progress are stored

#

Activated licenses and your learning progress are stored only in your own browser. Clearing your browser's site data removes them from that device. Use Export backup in My Guides to keep a copy, and Import backup to restore it on any device.

Lost licenses

#

If you lose your license code, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and the email address you used at checkout, and we'll send your code again.

How long you have access

#

Online access to the guides continues for as long as Shortcut Press operates. Once a guide has been opened in a browser, it also works offline on that device. When a guide is revised because software has changed, the revised version appears the next time you open it.

No physical shipment

#

Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

Questions

#

For anything else about delivery, contact us through the Contact page.
