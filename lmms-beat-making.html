An interactive browser guide to making a complete beat in LMMS, the free music production program: the workspace, programming drums in the Beat+Bassline Editor, instruments and the Piano Roll, arranging a song, mixing with the FX Mixer, and automation and export, with drum samples, MIDI chords and bass, and a song plan.

What the guide covers

  1. 01

    The LMMS workspace

    Find the main editors, set the tempo, load samples from the sidebar, and play your first sounds.

  2. 02

    Programming drums

    Load samples into the Beat+Bassline Editor, program a 16-step pattern, and add feel with volume and variation.

  3. 03

    Instruments and the Piano Roll

    Add a synthesizer, choose and tweak presets, draw and edit notes in the Piano Roll, and import MIDI files.

  4. 04

    Arranging a song

    Place patterns in the Song Editor, build an intro, main section and outro, and use loop points while you work.

  5. 05

    Mixing with the FX Mixer

    Send each instrument to its own mixer channel, balance levels and panning, and add effects such as EQ, reverb and compression.

  6. 06

    Automation and export

    Automate a filter or volume over time, draw curves in the Automation Editor, and export the finished track to WAV and MP3.

Software required

This guide is written for LMMS 1.2, the free and open-source music production program for Windows, Mac and Linux, obtained separately from lmms.io; it is not included with the guide. Headphones help; a MIDI keyboard is optional, since every note can be drawn with the mouse.

About software updates. Software makers update their programs, so menus and button locations can change between versions. This guide is revised when an update changes the steps, and the revised version appears the next time you open it.

What you need

  • A modern web browser (such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • LMMS 1.2 (free and open source, for Windows, Mac and Linux), obtained separately, to follow the steps.
  • An internet connection the first time you open the guide on a device; after that it also works offline.

How the guide works

This is a self-study guide. You read each step, carry it out in your own copy of the software, check your understanding with the quiz, then try the exercise and compare your work with the worked solution. There are no video lessons, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work.

Is this guide a good fit?

Who it's for

  • You'd like to make beats or simple tracks and have never used music software.
  • You've opened LMMS and didn't know where to start.
  • You want to understand drum patterns, chords and arrangement by building them.

Who it isn't for

  • You need to record and mix live instruments and vocals in depth. Audacity or a full DAW suits that better.
  • You want music theory in depth; the guide teaches just enough to make a beat.
  • You want video lessons, live help, or someone to review your work.
