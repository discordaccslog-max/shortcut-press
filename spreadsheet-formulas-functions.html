What the guide covers

  1. Formulas and cell references

    How a formula is put together, the difference between relative, absolute and mixed references, and what happens to each kind when you copy a formula into other cells.

  2. Lookups

    Finding a value in one table and returning the matching value from another, exact and approximate matches, and lookups that can return a result from any column.

  3. Logical functions

    IF on its own and with AND and OR, handling more than two outcomes, and counting or adding up only the rows that meet a condition.

  4. Text functions

    Joining and splitting text, trimming stray spaces, changing case, and pulling one part of a value out of a longer piece of text.

  5. Date functions

    How spreadsheets store dates, working out the time between two dates, adding days and months, and formatting dates so they display the way you want.

  6. Error handling

    What the common error values mean, tracing a formula back to the cell that causes a problem, and returning a clean result when a formula cannot calculate.

  7. Excel and Google Sheets side by side

    Where a step or a function works differently in the two programs, the guide shows both versions so you can follow along in whichever one you use.

This is a self-study guide. You read each step, carry it out in your own copy of the software, then do the exercises and compare your work with the worked solutions. There is no video, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work. Everything you get is in the .zip.
