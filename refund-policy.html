Refund Policy

Every purchase can be refunded in full within 14 days.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short

  • Full refund on any purchase within 14 days, no reason required.
  • Request it through the Contact page with your order number.
  • Issued to your original payment method within 5 business days.
  • Nothing needs to be returned.

Full refunds within 14 days

You can get a full refund on any Shortcut Press purchase if you request it within 14 days of buying. You don't need to give a reason. This applies to every product, including the Complete Software Skills Library.

How to request a refund

Contact us through the Contact page and include your order number. Including the email address you used at checkout helps us find your order quickly.

When you'll receive it

Refunds are made to your original payment method and are issued within 5 business days of your request. Your payment provider's own processing time may add a few days before the money appears in your account.

Nothing to return

Because the products are digital downloads, nothing needs to be returned.

Damaged files or files that won't open

If a file you downloaded is damaged or won't open, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and a short description of the problem. If you'd prefer a working replacement rather than a refund, a replacement will be sent to you instead.

Questions

If anything about this policy is unclear, contact us through the Contact page.
