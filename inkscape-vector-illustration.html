An interactive browser guide to drawing clean vector artwork in Inkscape: shapes, paths, the pen tool, text, and exporting for print and web, with downloadable SVG practice files.

What the guide covers

  1. 01

    Shapes

    Draw rectangles, circles, stars and polygons, set fill and stroke, and arrange, align and group objects.

  2. 02

    Paths

    Convert shapes to paths, edit nodes, and combine shapes with union, difference and intersection.

  3. 03

    The pen tool

    Draw straight and curved segments, adjust nodes and handles, and trace over a sketch.

  4. 04

    Text

    Add and style text, place text along a path, and convert text to paths when a file needs it.

  5. 05

    Exporting for print and web

    Set document size and units, export PNG images at the size you need, save SVG files for the web, and save PDF files for print.

This guide uses Inkscape. Inkscape is not included with the guide; you need your own copy, obtained separately from its maker.

What you need

  • A modern web browser (such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • Inkscape, obtained separately, to follow the steps.
  • An internet connection the first time you open the guide on a device; after that it also works offline.

How the guide works

This is a self-study guide. You read each step, carry it out in your own copy of the software, check your understanding with the quiz, then try the exercise and compare your work with the worked solution. There are no video lessons, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work.

Is this guide a good fit?

Who it's for

  • You are new to Inkscape or to vector drawing in general.
  • You want to make icons, simple illustrations, diagrams or graphics that stay sharp at any size.
  • You want to understand the pen tool instead of avoiding it.

Who it isn't for

  • You want photo editing or digital painting. Those are not covered.
  • You need professional print production or prepress. That is not covered.
  • You want video lessons, live help, or someone to review your work.
