An interactive browser guide to recording and streaming with OBS Studio: setup and the OBS window, screen, camera and overlay sources, clean and consistent audio with filters, scenes, transitions and hotkeys, crash-safe recording, and streaming safely and using the virtual camera, with overlays, a test tone and a show plan.

What the guide covers

  1. 01

    Setup and the OBS window

    Run the auto-configuration wizard, set the canvas size and frame rate, and learn the scenes, sources, mixer and controls.

  2. 02

    Screen, camera and overlays

    Capture a screen or a window, add a webcam with a frame, place image and text overlays, and size and crop sources precisely.

  3. 03

    Clean, consistent audio

    Set microphone and desktop audio levels, add noise suppression, a gate, a compressor and a limiter, and monitor what you're sending.

  4. 04

    Scenes, transitions and hotkeys

    Build the show's scenes, reuse sources between them, choose transitions, prepare changes in Studio Mode, and switch with hotkeys.

  5. 05

    Recording

    Choose where and how recordings are saved, pick a safe format and the right encoder, record, and convert to MP4.

  6. 06

    Streaming and the virtual camera

    Connect a streaming service safely, choose bitrate and resolution, check performance with the Stats dock, and use the virtual camera in video calls.

Software required

This guide is written for OBS Studio 30 or later, free and open source for Windows, Mac and Linux, obtained separately from obsproject.com; it is not included with the guide. You'll want a webcam and a microphone for some exercises, though the practice files let you build every scene without them. Streaming needs an account with a streaming service, which the guide doesn't provide.

About software updates. Software makers update their programs, so menus and button locations can change between versions. This guide is revised when an update changes the steps, and the revised version appears the next time you open it.

What you need

  • A modern web browser (such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • OBS Studio 30 or later (free and open source, for Windows, Mac and Linux), obtained separately, to follow the steps.
  • An internet connection the first time you open the guide on a device; after that it also works offline.

How the guide works

This is a self-study guide. You read each step, carry it out in your own copy of the software, check your understanding with the quiz, then try the exercise and compare your work with the worked solution. There are no video lessons, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work.

Is this guide a good fit?

Who it's for

  • You want to record clean screen tutorials, presentations or gameplay.
  • You plan to stream and want your setup right before going live.
  • You've installed OBS and found scenes, sources and settings confusing.

Who it isn't for

  • You need to edit recordings. See Video Editing in DaVinci Resolve.
  • You want growth or monetization advice for a channel; the guide is about the software.
  • You want video lessons, live help, or someone to review your work.
