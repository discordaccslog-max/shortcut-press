Consumer Rights

Your rights when you buy from Shortcut Press, including information for customers in the EU and UK.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short

#
  • You can cancel any purchase and get a full refund within 14 days, without giving a reason.
  • Your statutory rights for digital content that is faulty or not as described are not affected by any of our policies.
  • Each purchase is a one-time payment. There are no subscriptions and nothing renews automatically.

Who you're buying from

#

You're buying from Shortcut Press. You can reach us through the Contact page, and we reply within two business days.

What you're buying

#

Each product is digital content: an interactive guide that runs in your web browser and is unlocked with a license code. The main characteristics, contents, level and software covered are described on each product's page before you buy.

  • What it works on: current versions of common web browsers (such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari) on a computer, tablet or phone, with JavaScript and site storage allowed.
  • What it doesn't include: the software each guide teaches, which you obtain separately from its maker.
  • How it's supplied: immediately after payment, on your order confirmation page, as described in the Delivery Policy.
  • How long it lasts: online access continues for as long as Shortcut Press operates, and guides you've opened also work offline on that device.

Price

#

Prices are shown in US dollars on each product page. The total you pay, including any applicable taxes, is shown at checkout before you confirm your payment.

Your right to cancel

#

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, consumer law normally gives you 14 days to cancel a purchase made online. For digital content supplied straight away, that right can end once supply begins, if you agree to it. Shortcut Press doesn't ask you to give up that right: you can cancel any purchase within 14 days of buying and receive a full refund, whether or not you've started using the guide, and without giving a reason. This applies to every customer, wherever you live.

To cancel, contact us through the Contact page and tell us that you're cancelling, which product, your order number and date, and the name and email address you used at checkout. Your refund is issued to your original payment method within 5 business days. The details are in the Refund Policy.

If something is faulty or not as described

#

If a guide or practice file is faulty, doesn't work as described, or doesn't match its description, contact us through the Contact page. We'll fix it or replace what's needed, and where that isn't possible, refund you. In the United Kingdom, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you rights for digital content; in the European Union, the rules on digital content give you similar rights. Nothing in our policies limits those statutory rights.

Updates

#

Software makers change their programs over time. When an update changes the steps in a guide, the guide is revised, and you see the revised version the next time you open it at no extra cost.

Questions and complaints

#

If you have a question or complaint about a purchase, contact us through the Contact page with your order number. We reply within two business days.
