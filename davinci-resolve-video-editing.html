An interactive browser guide to editing a video from raw clips to a finished file in DaVinci Resolve: importing and organizing footage, cutting on the timeline, transitions, titles, basic color correction, audio cleanup, and exporting, with downloadable practice clips.

What the guide covers

  1. 01

    Importing and organizing footage

    Set up a project, bring in clips, sort them into bins, and find the shot you need quickly.

  2. 02

    Cutting on the timeline

    Mark in and out points, place clips, trim, ripple delete, and work with more than one track.

  3. 03

    Transitions

    Add, adjust and remove transitions, and when a straight cut works better.

  4. 04

    Titles

    Add text titles and lower thirds, set their timing, and keep them readable.

  5. 05

    Basic color correction

    Adjust exposure and white balance, and make shots from the same scene look like they belong together.

  6. 06

    Audio cleanup

    Set levels, reduce background noise, add fades, and keep the main sound clear under music.

  7. 07

    Exporting

    Choose export settings for common uses and render the finished video.

What you need

  • A modern web browser (such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • DaVinci Resolve, obtained separately, to follow the steps.
  • An internet connection the first time you open the guide on a device; after that it also works offline.

How the guide works

This is a self-study guide. You read each step, carry it out in your own copy of the software, check your understanding with the quiz, then try the exercise and compare your work with the worked solution. There are no video lessons, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work.

Is this guide a good fit?

Who it's for

  • You are new to DaVinci Resolve, or new to video editing in general.
  • You have used a simpler editor and want a structured path through a full editing workflow.
  • You would rather read a step and try it than follow along with someone on screen.

Who it isn't for

  • You need visual effects compositing, advanced color grading, or motion graphics. Those are not covered.
  • You already edit regularly in DaVinci Resolve and want advanced techniques.
  • You want video lessons, live help, or someone to review your work.
