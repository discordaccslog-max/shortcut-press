A step-by-step guide to editing a video from raw clips to a finished file in DaVinci Resolve. It covers importing and organizing footage, cutting on the timeline, transitions, titles, basic color correction, audio cleanup, and exporting, with practice footage for every exercise.

What the guide covers

  1. 01

    Importing and organizing footage

    Setting up a project, bringing in clips, sorting them into bins, and finding the shot you need quickly.

  2. 02

    Cutting on the timeline

    Marking in and out points with I and O, placing clips, trimming, ripple edits, and working with more than one video and audio track.

  3. 03

    Transitions

    Adding, adjusting and removing transitions, and when a straight cut works better.

  4. 04

    Titles

    Adding text titles and lower thirds, setting their timing, and keeping them readable.

  5. 05

    Basic color correction

    Adjusting exposure and white balance, and making shots from the same scene look like they belong together.

  6. 06

    Audio cleanup

    Setting levels, reducing background noise, adding fades, and keeping dialogue clear under music.

  7. 07

    Exporting

    Choosing export settings for common uses and rendering the finished video.

How the guide works

This is a self-study guide. You read each step, carry it out in your own copy of the software, then do the exercises and compare your work with the worked solutions. There is no video, no live instruction, no tutoring, and no feedback on your work. Everything you get is in the .zip.

Is this guide a good fit?

Who it's for

  • You are new to DaVinci Resolve, or new to video editing in general.
  • You have used a simpler editor and want a structured path through a full editing workflow.
  • You would rather read a step and try it than follow along with someone on screen.

Who it isn't for

  • You need visual effects compositing, advanced color grading, or motion graphics. Those are not covered.
  • You already edit regularly in DaVinci Resolve and want advanced techniques.
  • You want video lessons, live help, or someone to review your work.
