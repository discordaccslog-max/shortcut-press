Privacy Policy

What information Shortcut Press receives, what stays on your device, and your rights.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short

#
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • Your licenses and learning progress are stored only in your own browser and are never sent anywhere.
  • Payments are handled by third-party payment providers. Shortcut Press never sees your full card details.
  • Your personal information is never sold or shared for advertising.

When you browse this website

#

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and Shortcut Press does not collect information about you while you browse it. Two outside services are involved in showing you the site:

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel and is also published through GitHub Pages. Like any web host, their servers receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, and each handles that information under its own privacy statement. Shortcut Press does not receive these server logs.
  • Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address as part of that request and handles it under its own privacy policy. Google Fonts does not set cookies on this site.

What's stored on your device

#

When you activate a license, the site stores the license code, your learning progress (lessons completed, quiz answers, where you left off) and your display settings in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is never sent to Shortcut Press or anyone else. Because we never receive it, we can't recover it if your browser's data is cleared, so please use Export backup in My Guides. The site also asks your browser to keep this data persistently so it isn't cleared automatically when space runs low.

When you buy a guide

#

Payments are handled by third-party payment providers. At checkout, the payment provider collects your name, email address, billing details and payment information under its own privacy policy. Shortcut Press never sees your full payment card details.

From each order, the payment provider gives Shortcut Press order information: your name, your email address, the products you bought, and your country.

How your information is used

#

Order information is used only to:

  • provide support, including sending a lost license code again;
  • process refunds; and
  • meet tax and accounting obligations.

It is not used for anything else, and you won't be added to a marketing list.

When you contact us

#

When you get in touch, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. That information is used only to answer you and resolve your request.

Sharing

#

Your personal information is never sold and never shared for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the store, such as the payment provider and our email provider, and only as far as they need it to provide that service. It may also be disclosed where the law requires it.

How long information is kept

#

Order information is kept for as long as legal and accounting requirements make it necessary, such as tax record-keeping rules, and is then deleted. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and handle any follow-up, and are then deleted. Information stored in your browser stays there until you remove it, for example by removing a license in My Guides or clearing the site's data.

Your rights

#

Under the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom)

#

You have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, where it isn't needed to meet a legal obligation;
  • object to how your information is used;
  • restrict how it's used and receive a copy in a portable format; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Order information is used to perform the contract you make when you buy a guide, to meet legal obligations such as tax record-keeping, and, for support, in our legitimate interest in answering your questions.

Under the CCPA (California)

#

California residents have the right to know what personal information is held about them and how it's used, to access it, to correct it, to request its deletion, and to limit its use. Shortcut Press does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You won't be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

How to exercise your rights

#

To make any request about your personal information, contact us through the Contact page. To protect your information, we may ask you to confirm a detail of your order, such as the email address you used at checkout. Requests are answered within the time the law requires. Information stored only in your browser is under your control; you can view it in My Guides, export it, or delete it at any time.

Children

#

This website is not directed at children under 13, and Shortcut Press does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the Contact page and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

#

When this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how information from past orders is used, customers will be told by email before it takes effect.

Questions

#

For any question about this policy or your information, contact us through the Contact page.
