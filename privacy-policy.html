Privacy Policy

What information Shortcut Press receives, how it is used, and your rights.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

When you browse this website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no forms. Shortcut Press does not collect any information about you while you browse it.

Two outside services are involved in showing you the site, and you should know about them:

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, and GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement. Shortcut Press does not receive these server logs.
  • Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request and handles it under its own privacy policy. Google Fonts does not set cookies on this site.

When you buy a guide

Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider. At checkout, the payment provider collects your name, email address, billing details and payment information under its own privacy policy. Shortcut Press never sees your full payment card details.

From each order, Shortcut Press receives order information: your name, your email address, the products you bought, and your country.

How your information is used

Order information is used only to:

  • deliver the products you bought, including updated files when a guide is revised;
  • provide support when you get in touch;
  • process refunds; and
  • meet tax and accounting obligations.

It is not used for anything else. You won't be added to a marketing list.

When you contact us

When you get in touch, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. That information is used only to answer you and to resolve your request.

Sharing

Your personal information is never sold, and it is never shared for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the store, such as the payment provider and the email service used to send download links and replies, and only to the extent they need it to provide that service. It may also be disclosed where the law requires it.

How long information is kept

Order information is kept for as long as legal and accounting requirements make it necessary, for example tax record-keeping rules, and is then deleted. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and handle any follow-up, and are then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, where it is not needed to meet a legal obligation;
  • object to how your information is used;
  • restrict how your information is used, and receive a copy of it in a portable format; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Order information is used to fulfil the contract you make when you buy a guide, to meet legal obligations such as tax record-keeping, and, for support, in the legitimate interest of answering your questions.

Under the CCPA (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is held about you and how it is used, to access it, to correct it, to request its deletion, and to object to or limit its use. Shortcut Press does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You will not be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

How to exercise your rights

To make any request about your personal information, contact us through the Contact page. To protect your information, we may ask you to confirm a detail of your order, such as the email address you used at checkout, before acting on a request. Requests are answered within the time the law requires.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and Shortcut Press does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the Contact page and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how information from past orders is used, customers will be told by email before the change takes effect.

Questions

For any question about this policy or your information, contact us through the Contact page.
