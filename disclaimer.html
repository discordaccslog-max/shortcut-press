Disclaimer

What the guides are, what they aren't, and what to keep in mind.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Self-study materials

Shortcut Press guides are self-study materials. They are not tutoring, and they include no feedback on anyone's work. There is no video and no live instruction.

Results vary

What you get out of a guide depends on your starting experience, the time you put in, and other individual factors. No specific result is promised from using any guide.

Software changes over time

Software makers change their programs over time. Steps in a guide may differ slightly in newer versions of a program until the guide is revised. When a guide is revised, updated files are free to anyone who bought it.

Keep backups

Always keep backups of your own files before practicing techniques on them. The practice files included with each guide are there so you can practice without touching your own work.

Trademarks

All software names mentioned on this website and in the guides are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used only to identify the programs the guides cover.

Independence

Shortcut Press is independent. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any software maker, and the guides are not official materials of any software maker.

Questions

If you have a question about this disclaimer, contact us through the Contact page.
